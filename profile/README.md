## 🖌️ Meet the Canvas

Name's Qooper. I've got paint on my hands and a whole server's worth of ideas I haven't finished yet — which is exactly where you come in.

CrayonSMP isn't a server I built *for* you. It's one I'm building *with* you. Every season the map changes shape, and the people who show up are the ones who decide what it becomes.

## 🧵 Two Ways In

### ⚙️ Build the Machine

Know your way around Java? I need hands on the actual guts of this place — plugins, systems, the stuff that makes the world tick.

We're open-source top to bottom. Claim a repo, ship a plugin, break things, fix them, repeat. No gatekeeping, just commits.

* 🔧 Runs on **CraftEngine**, **ModelEngine**, **MythicMobs**, and a growing pile of GitHub integrations.
* 🚪 **Walk in here:** [Development Guidelines and open Issues](https://github.com/CrayonSMP)

### 🌍 Live in the World

Prefer surviving to coding? There's a spot on the server for you too. Whitelist only, 16+, kept that way so the world stays worth building.

* 📝 **Apply through Discord** — that's the only door.
